# Kostiantynivka: Line of Contact Assessment

An open-source GEOINT assessment of how the fighting reshaped the eastern approaches to Kostiantynivka, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, built entirely from Google Earth historical imagery (July 2020 baseline, September 2024 latest capture).

**View the interactive assessment:** https://macrovola.github.io/Kostiantynivka/

## What it covers

- **Bottom line up front** with confidence-rated judgments (ICD 203 style)
- **Terrain mapping:** 2020 vs. 2024 comparison of the city's eastern approaches
- **Detected change and movement:** annotated imagery of trench lines, burn scars, an active grass fire, impact craters, and vehicle tracks, filterable by GEOINT's three contributions to situational awareness (Murdock & Clark, 2015)
- **Assessment:** judgments, an alternative explanation, and indicators that would change them
- **Filling the gaps:** the collection and partner sources needed for real-time awareness

## Key finding

The newest public imagery of this sector is about two years older than the assessment. Free archives can document the war's environmental footprint, but they cannot provide current situational awareness; that requires tasked collection with frequent revisit.
